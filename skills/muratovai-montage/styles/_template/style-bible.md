# Style bible: <creator> (the skeleton every style profile follows)

`analyze_reference.py report` writes a draft with these sections and the measured numbers filled in (`style-bible.draft.md`).
Finish every TODO from the contact sheets, full-size frames and your beat sheets, then save it as `style-bible.md` in the profile.
The planner reads it before every plan in this style.

## 1. Passport
Duration, words per second, first visual change, beat length, layout run, loudness, music under the voice, SFX per 10 s.

## 2. Layouts
Shares of split / full-frame graphic / face; the shape of the split (arch with the head crossing its top edge = era 2026, straight
50/50 seam with boxed captions = era 2025); what each layout is used for; order rules.

## 3. Cuts and transitions
Hard cuts or soft moves (`meta.trans`), where a cut sits against the word, special moves (flash, light leak, zoom-in start).

## 4. Captions
Words per chunk, font per layout, colour, height, which words get the accent, the CTA keyword look.

## 5. Colour
The accent and where it is allowed; backgrounds and what each one means.

## 6. Fonts
The closest bundled fonts for each caption and title style.

## 7. Motion
Entrances, how long anything stays still, how logos, numbers and interfaces are timed against the words.

## 8. Sound
Opening sound, what gets a whoosh / pop / click, music genre and level, density; `meta.profile` bold or measured.

## 9. The hook and the structure
What the first 3 seconds show, the skeleton of a typical reel, the CTA.

## 10. Rules for the planner
10–20 numbered rules, each with numbers ("the first layout change by 2 s", "never two faces in a row").
